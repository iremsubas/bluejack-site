FAQ

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?
Sign in with the same email (My Table → Account). Guest progress is tied to that phone, so link an email first.

How do I play with a friend?
Share your friend code (My Table or Social → Friends). Your friend adds it, you accept, then tap 1v1 next to their name in Social.

How do I report or block someone?
Tap the player's name in a leaderboard, friend list or match, then choose Report or Block. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?
My Table → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and data. See the Privacy Policy.

Is this real gambling?
No. Bluejack uses in-game chips and gems only. Nothing in the game can be cashed out for money or prizes.
