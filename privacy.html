Bluejack Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

HomeSupport

Bluejack is a PvP blackjack game. This policy explains what data the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. Bluejack does not sell your data, does not show ads, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Account: if you sign in with email, your email address (used only to send your sign-in code and to restore your account). You can also play as a guest without giving an email.
  • Player profile: a random account ID, your display name, your 6-character friend code, avatar style, trophies, match counts, club name and when you were last online. Your display name, avatar, trophies and friend code are visible to other players (for example on leaderboards and friend lists).
  • Game progress: your save (gems, cards, emotes, Battle Pass progress and similar), so it is kept when you change phones.
  • Social: friend requests, friends, players you block, tournament entries and results.
  • Reports: if you report a player, we store who reported whom, the reason and the time, so we can review it.
  • Gameplay analytics: a short list of game events (for example app opened, match started or finished, tutorial finished) with your account ID, language and platform. We use this only to understand and improve the game. No session recordings, no screen capture, and no IP address is stored with these events.

What we don't collect

No location, contacts, photos, microphone, advertising identifier or payment card details. Match chat inside clubs is simulated in the game and is not sent to our servers.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts, profiles, saves and social data, and sends sign-in code emails.
  • PostHog (EU servers) receives the gameplay analytics events described above.
  • Apple processes any App Store purchases. We never see your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game schedules reminders on your phone (for example when a chest is ready). These are created on your device; no push token is sent to us.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the game: My Table → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, save, friends, blocks, reports you made and tournament entries from our servers. Analytics events tied to your account ID are deleted on request (see below).

No real gambling

Bluejack is for adults 18 and over. You never bet gems, coins or money: rounds are played for hearts, and nothing in the game can be cashed out for money or prizes.

Children

Bluejack is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights and contact

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion. Contact us through the Support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
